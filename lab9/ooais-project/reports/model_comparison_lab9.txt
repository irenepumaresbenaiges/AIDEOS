MODEL COMPARISON
================

1. SimpleCNN from scratch
Accuracy: 0.8056
Training time: 1.4681 seconds
Comments: Lower performance than classical ML (Random Forest) in this small dataset. It struggles with mixed textures like rivers surrounded by forest.

2. ResNet18 frozen feature extractor
Accuracy: 0.9722
Training time: 18.46 seconds
Comments: Excellent performance. Reusing ImageNet features is much more effective than training a small CNN from scratch, even with the weights frozen.

3. ResNet18 fine-tuned last block
Accuracy: 1.0000
Training time: 24.74 seconds
Comments: Perfect classification. Allowing the last block (layer4) to adapt to satellite-specific spatial features resolved the remaining confusion in the "River" class.



BEST ACCURACY:
ResNet18 fine-tuned last block

SimpleCNN
