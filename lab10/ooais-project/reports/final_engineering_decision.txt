FINAL ENGINEERING DECISION REPORT

Selected Model: ResNet18 with fine-tuning (last block).

1. Technical Justification
Based on the comparative analysis, the fine-tened ResNet18 is the BEST choice. While the SimpleCNN from scratch only achieved ~80% accuracy and the frozen ResNet18 reached 97.22%, the fine-tuned version achieved a score of 100% accuracy. By allowing the weights of layer4 to adapt, the model successfully learned specific satellite spatial features that the generic ImageNet weights could not fully capture.

2. Expected Advantages
- Maximum Reliability: The model demonstrated perfect class separation in the confusion matrix.
- Feature Adaptation: Unlike the frozen model, fine-tuning allows the high-level filters to specialize in orbital perspectives.
- Transfer of Knowledge: It leverages millions of images from ImageNet, requiring just a few hundreds of local images to reach good performance.
- Stable Convergence: As seen in the training logs, the loss decreases consistently, ensuring a predictable training process.

3. Possible Risks
- Overfitting: With such a small dataset, there is a risk that the model learns the specific noise of these images.
- Computational Cost: Training time increased by ~35% compared to the frozen model (from 18s to 24s). Here the difference is negligible, but it could increase with larger datasets.
- Unknown: The model still forces unknown inputs  into known classes with high confidence, requiring an external rejection threshold.

4. Deployment Considerations
- The deployment environment must include an exact replica of the ImageNet normalization and 224x224 resizing; otherwise, accuracy will degrade significantly.
- Implement a "Confidence Threshold" (e.g., 0.90). If a prediction falls below this, it should be flagged for human review to mitigate the risk of misclassifying unknown land covers.

Engineering Questions
- Classification Accuracy: Fine-tuned ResNet18 (1.00) > Frozen ResNet18 (0.97) > SimpleCNN (0.80).
- Training Time: Although slower than SimpleCNN, the 24-second training time is extremely efficient for the accuracy gained.
- Implementation Complexity: Low. Using torchvision.models makes it easier to maintain than a custom CNN architecture.
- Risk of overfitting: With small datasets.
- Robustness on unknown inputs: It is possible to solve this by implementing a confidence threshold. 
- Suitability for Small Datasets: Excellent. Transfer learning is the only viable strategy when only a few hundred images are available per class.
- Operational Reliability: High. Grad-CAM visualizations confirm the model focuses on relevant land-cover patterns and not on background noise.
