FINE TUNING ANALYSIS

• Did fine-tuning improve accuracy? Yes
• Did training become slower? Yes

• Is fine-tuning worth the additional computational cost? Yes
• Would you use the frozen model or the fine-tuned model in a small-data satellite classification problem? I would choose the fine-tuned model. Even though the frozen model is faster to train and computationally cheaper, the fine-tuning approach demonstrated a clear improvement in accuracy. Since the dataset is small, the extra training time is still relatively short, making the trade-off for higher reliability and better class separation highly beneficial.
